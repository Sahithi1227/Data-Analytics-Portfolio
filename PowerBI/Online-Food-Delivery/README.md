# Online Food Delivery Analytics | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze an online food delivery dataset containing 50,000 orders.

The dashboard explores order patterns, customer behavior, restaurant performance, delivery operations, payment methods, weather conditions and order priorities to understand different factors affecting food delivery operations.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 50,000 food delivery orders with information related to:

- Order details
- Customer age and type
- Restaurant type and category
- Order priority
- Weather conditions
- Time of day
- Day of the week
- Payment methods
- Delivery partner experience and ratings
- Preparation time
- Delivery status
- Late deliveries
- City and delivery areas

## Dashboard Analysis

### Page 1 – Order Overview

- Analyzed total order count across the dataset.
- Examined orders across different days of the week and delivery areas.
- Analyzed orders based on restaurant type.
- Added weather type and time-of-day filters for interactive analysis.

### Page 2 – Order Priority & Restaurant Analysis

- Analyzed order priority based on the number of items ordered.
- Studied item count across different times of the day.
- Analyzed restaurant types according to different time periods.
- Added day-of-week filters for interactive exploration.

### Page 3 – Customer & Delivery Analysis

- Analyzed food orders based on customer age.
- Identified late delivery counts.
- Examined orders based on restaurant primary categories.
- Analyzed the relationship between restaurant ratings and order counts.
- Used customer age as an interactive filter.

### Page 4 – Payment, Weather & Restaurant Analysis

- Analyzed payment method distribution across different times of the day.
- Calculated average preparation time for food items.
- Examined order delivery patterns under different weather conditions.
- Compared restaurant primary categories across restaurant types.
- Added customer rating filters for deeper analysis.

### Page 5 – Customer & Delivery Partner Analysis

- Analyzed total orders and maximum tip amount.
- Examined customer types such as New and Premium.
- Analyzed food orders across customer age groups.
- Identified areas with higher item-order activity.
- Studied delivery partner assignment based on customer type and experience.
- Analyzed delivery partner ratings.
- Compared customer types across different days of the week.
