# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive HR Analytics dashboard developed using Power BI to explore employee demographics, job roles, departments, business travel, education, experience, attrition, distance and income-related metrics.

The dashboard consists of 5 pages with interactive filters and visualizations that allow different aspects of the workforce data to be explored.

## Dataset

The dataset contains information on 1,470 employees, including:

- Gender
- Marital Status
- Education
- Job Role
- Department
- Business Travel
- Distance
- Employee Involvement
- Experience
- Attrition
- Income

## Key Analysis

The dashboard focuses on:

- Employee count and workforce distribution
- Gender and marital status distribution
- Education status
- Job role distribution
- Department-wise employee distribution
- Business travel patterns
- Employee involvement
- Attrition across gender and departments
- Department-wise experienced employees
- Education-wise experience
- Job role and business travel patterns
- Distance categories
- Employee income metrics
